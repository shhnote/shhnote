## The short version

We can't read your secrets. They are encrypted on your device, and the key never reaches us. We keep as little else as we can, and delete it as soon as we can.

## What we store

When you create a secret, we store:

- **The encrypted secret.** Without the key in your link, it is unreadable, including to us.
- **Its settings:** when it expires, whether it should be deleted after reading, and whether a passphrase is needed.
- **Basic facts about it:** its size and when it was created.

We don't store your passphrase or the key from your link.

## When we delete it

A secret is deleted when it expires, or straight after it has been opened if burn after reading is on. Deleted secrets can't be recovered.

## What we don't do

- We don't use cookies or tracking, and we don't show ads.
- We don't ask you to create an account.
- We don't sell or share your data.
