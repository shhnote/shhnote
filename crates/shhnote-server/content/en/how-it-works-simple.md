## 1. You write your secret

Type a note, add files, or both. Choose how long the link should work, whether it should disappear after the first view, and optionally add a passphrase.

## 2. Your browser locks it

Before anything is sent, your browser locks your secret with a new, random key. Only the locked version is uploaded, so we can't read it.

## 3. You share the link

You get a link that contains the key. Send it to the person you want to share with. If you set a passphrase, tell them that separately.

## 4. They open it

When they open the link and press **Reveal secret**, their browser downloads the locked secret and unlocks it with the key from the link.

## 5. It disappears

If you chose burn after reading, the secret is deleted as soon as it's opened. Either way, it's deleted when it expires. After that, the link doesn't work for anyone, including you.

**Why is there a reveal button?** Chat apps often open links in the background to show a preview. The button makes sure the secret is only opened by a person.
